### The Question

We live in a strange era. It feels like anyone can build an app in a few hours, reach $1M ARR in a month, or "change or ruin their life in one day." Society is obsessed with these stories—maximum productivity, leverage, acceleration. Build something others will buy, then sell it to even more people.

I've gradually realized that I don't quite fit into it.

There's a quiet truth behind most successful products: the majority of things that sell well are boring businesses. And when something sells well *and* isn't boring, everyone is already doing it.

Recently, while watching *Culinary Class Wars* Season 2 on Netflix, the final mission caught me off guard: cook a dish for yourself. Many of these chefs rarely cook for themselves—their entire careers revolve around pleasing others, satisfying judges, customers, or abstract expectations.

That question stayed with me. When was the last time I built something purely for myself?

---

### What It Is

Despite the name, Acid Music Player isn't a general-purpose music player. It's specifically a **Mixcloud player**.

While most people rely on Spotify or Apple Music, I've never been into recommendation algorithms (ironic, since I once worked as a music recommendation engineer). These days, I mostly listen to radio stations like NTS Live—open-ended, genre-agnostic, and unpredictable.

The friction comes from discovery and memory:
- Constantly checking NTS, LYL, and other radio platforms for new shows
- Wanting to collect not entire episodes, but **specific moments**—strange, beautiful, or surprising enough to keep

There's also a more personal motivation: I like weird things. Y2K Winamp skins, strange MP3 shapes, slightly uncomfortable interfaces. I never understood why everyone should experience music through the same clean, neutral Spotify UI.

---

### Features

- Manage the Mixcloud accounts you follow
- Check their latest episodes
- Group shows by tags
- **Favorite moments** rather than entire tracks
- Evolving shader visuals controlled by volume
- Every listening session looks slightly different

---

### Technical Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|------------|
| Core Functionality | Claude Code (Opus 4.5) |
| Shader Visuals | Gemini 3 Pro |
| Platform | macOS native app |

The visual style is heavily inspired by **Showa-era Ultraman aesthetics**—dreamy, fluid, and slightly surreal. I'm not good at writing shaders myself, but AI is now capable enough that taste and intention matter more than technical mastery.

---

### Why It Matters

This isn't a startup. It isn't optimized for growth, monetization, or distribution. It's simply something I wanted to exist.

For the first time in a long while, I'm building without asking whether it will sell or whether it could help others. I'm only asking: **would I actually use it?**

This is the first project in a small series about building things just for myself.

*More to come.*

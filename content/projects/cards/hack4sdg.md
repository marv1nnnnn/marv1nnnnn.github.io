Workshop at the Hack4SDG Workshop Series hosted by City University of Hong Kong on September 25, 2025. The session introduced Vibe Coding as an innovative method for turning abstract ideas into working prototypes using generative AI, regardless of technical background.

**Event:** [Hack4SDG Workshop Series @ CityUHK](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/ted/workshops/20250925_hack4sdg/)

**Resources** [Slide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Ru9hm2gPq-ru-X1n0k3-72U8pUFI_uG78g48aMqFW30)

**Venue:** LI-4412, Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Academic Building, City University of Hong Kong

![Hack4sdg](/images/hack4sdg.jpg)

### Highlights
- Introduced the concept of "Vibe Coding" - using generative AI to transform feelings and ideas into interactive websites.
- Shared personal journey from physics and acoustics background to becoming a programmer and Cursor Ambassador.
- Demonstrated how AI-assisted coding has democratized software development, making it accessible to everyone.
- Showcased creative examples like Infinite Wiki and gesture-controlled interfaces that were impossible before LLMs.
- Explained the "pancake cooking" analogy for understanding how to work effectively with AI coding agents.

### Reflection
- The workshop highlighted how coding has evolved from a privilege to a universal creative tool.
- Participants learned that the key to successful AI-assisted development lies in clear communication, proper context, and developing good "taste."
- Reinforced that creativity and imagination are now the primary limits, not technical barriers.
- Showed how YouWare's platform enables rapid prototyping and iteration while maintaining focus on core concepts.

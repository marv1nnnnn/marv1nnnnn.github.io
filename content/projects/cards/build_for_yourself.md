> The fastest way to build something meaningful for everyone might be to begin by building for yourself.

**Link:** [youbuild-hackathon-marvin.youware.app](https://youbuild-hackathon-marvin.youware.app/)

---

### The Question

We're always coding for work, for deadlines, for someone else.

When was the last time you made something just because you wanted it to exist?

---

### The Inspiration

The theme came from watching *Culinary Class Wars* Season 2 on Netflix. The final mission caught me off guard: cook a dish for yourself.

Many of these chefs rarely cook for themselves—their entire careers revolve around pleasing others, satisfying judges, customers, or abstract expectations.

That question stayed with me. When was the last time I built something purely for myself?

---

### The Philosophy

Some of the greatest products came from builders scratching their own itch:

- **Cursor** — Michael & team built the AI-native dev environment they personally wanted to use every day
- **Claude Code** — Boris's personal quest for a terminal-based music player evolved into a breakthrough in CLI-based AI interaction
- **Shopify** — Tobi built the commerce engine he needed while selling snowboards, now powering millions of stores

When you build for yourself, you understand the problem deeply.

---

### The Message

Building for everyone is the fastest way to build for no one.

Building for yourself is the fastest way to build for everyone.

Start building your own scratch.

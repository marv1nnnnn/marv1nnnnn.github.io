> This is the first speech delivered at Cursor Meetup Shenzhen, marking the very first Cursor community meetup in China.

**Slides & Source:** [marvin-cursor-share](https://marvin-cursor-share.vercel.app/1)

![Cursor Shenzhen](/images/cursor_shenzhen.png)

---

Cursor started as a tool for programmers, but it’s become a community—a place where we share, learn, and build together. In this talk, I reflected on:

- **Yesterday:** The origins of Cursor, the challenges we faced, and the milestones we’ve achieved as a global community.
- **Today:** How Cursor empowers developers in China and worldwide, and the vibrant ecosystem that’s emerging.
- **Tomorrow:** Our vision for the future—open collaboration, new features, and a growing network of Cursor enthusiasts.

### Highlights

- Shared stories from early adopters and contributors.
- Discussed the importance of open-source and community-driven development.
- Outlined upcoming initiatives and ways to get involved.

### Looking Forward

- Building more local meetups and workshops across China.
- Fostering collaboration between Cursor users, contributors, and the broader dev community.
- Continuing to innovate and make programming more accessible for everyone.

---

Thank you to everyone who joined us for this historic first meetup in Shenzhen. Let’s keep building the future of Cursor—together!

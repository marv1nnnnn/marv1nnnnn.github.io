Invited workshop and talk at Adventure X, the largest hackathon in China, held on July 26, 2025. The session focused on rapid prototyping, improvisational design, and the power of 'vibe' in collaborative creative processes.

**Link:** [marv1nnnnn.github.io/adventurex_vibe](https://marv1nnnnn.github.io/adventurex_vibe/)

![Vibe is All You Need](/images/vibe_is_all_you_need.png)

### Highlights
- Explored the concept of 'vibe' as a guiding principle for team creativity and project direction.
- Led participants through live prototyping exercises using web-based tools and generative art techniques.
- Shared stories and lessons from previous hackathons, emphasizing intuition, play, and serendipity.
- Facilitated group activities to break creative blocks and encourage spontaneous collaboration.

### Reflection
- The workshop reinforced that technical skills are important, but the collective energy and openness of a team often determine the success of a project.
- Participants left with new strategies for embracing uncertainty and letting the 'vibe' lead their creative process.

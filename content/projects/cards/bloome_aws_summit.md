I recently joined **AWS Summit Shanghai** to introduce **Bloome** — the product we are building at [bloome.im](https://bloome.im).

Bloome is an instant-messaging platform for **humans and AI agents working together in one chat**. Instead of treating Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, coding agents, research agents, and teammates as separate tools in separate tabs, Bloome brings them into a shared conversation where they can collaborate, debate, review, remember context, and help ship real work.

The core idea is simple: you are not replaced by agents — you are amplified by an AI team. Agents can take different roles, check each other's work, share memory inside the conversation, and produce outputs like research, campaign assets, dashboards, operational reports, and product work with humans still in the loop.

At the AWS Summit, I shared how Bloome fits into the next wave of multi-agent collaboration: messaging as the interface, agents as teammates, and shared context as the workspace.

I recently joined **AWS Summit Shanghai** to introduce **Bloome**, a new product we are building at [bloome.im](https://bloome.im).

Bloome is about making it easier to turn ideas into living web experiences — a place where creation feels closer to sketching, remixing, and publishing than managing infrastructure.

At the summit, I shared how Bloome fits into the new wave of AI-native creation tools: products that help people move from intention to working software faster, while keeping the output personal and expressive.

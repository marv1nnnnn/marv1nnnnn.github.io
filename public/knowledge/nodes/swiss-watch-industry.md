# Swiss watch industry
Type: concept
Sources: https://paulgraham.com/brandage.html

Industry that underwent a major transformation during and after the quartz crisis, shifting from precision performance to luxury branding.

## Relations
- → Brand: "the way they did it was by transforming themselves from precision instrument makers into luxury brands."
- ← Quartz crisis: "In the early 1970s disaster struck the Swiss watch industry. Now people call it the quartz crisis"

# internal coordination costs
Type: concept
Sources: https://x.com/danhockenmaier/status/2034698196098978012

The organizational costs of managing activity within firms; the essay argues AI sharply lowers these costs by reducing required headcount and doing more work directly.

## Relations
- → economic concentration: "AI means many fewer people will be required to produce a unit of output. This will lower headcount, lower complexity, and cause internal coordination costs to plummet."
- ← AI adoption: "AI will do this much more powerfully than legacy software, because it won’t just give humans tools to manage more efficiently, it will do a lot of the work itself."

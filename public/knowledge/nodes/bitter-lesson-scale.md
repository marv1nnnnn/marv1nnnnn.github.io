# Bitter Lesson (Scale)
Type: concept
Sources: https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/scaffold/

Rich Sutton's thesis that scaling compute, data, and model capacity reliably beats clever engineering. Dominated AI strategy from 2017-2025 and drove the rise of OpenAI/Anthropic, but the author argues the second half of the decade calls for a different bet.

## Relations
- → Neural Language Model: "religiously following this bet is how companies like OpenAI and Anthropic have exploded in valuation since"
- → Evaluation Crisis: "the obsession over 'raw model capability' has ironically led to our evaluation metrics being completely off"

# distribution bottleneck
Type: concept
Sources: https://x.com/danhockenmaier/status/2034698196098978012

The idea that AI makes software easier to build but does not make distribution easier, making access to customers the key constraint.

## Relations
- → aggregators: "it will not make it easier to get distribution, and that will become the bottleneck, allowing aggregators to extract most of the value."

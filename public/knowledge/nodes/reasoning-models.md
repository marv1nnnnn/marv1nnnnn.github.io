# Reasoning models
Type: concept
Sources: https://nrehiew.github.io/blog/minimal_editing/

Models with extended reasoning that tend by default to elaborate and 'improve' code beyond the required fix, though they can also follow minimal-edit constraints well when explicitly instructed.

## Relations
- → Over-Editing: "Therefore, the takeaway is that the default behavior of most reasoning models is to over-edit."
- ← minimal editing prompt: "that same reasoning capacity also makes them better at following the constraint once it is given"

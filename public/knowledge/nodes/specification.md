# Specification
Type: concept
Sources: https://www.latent.space/p/reviews-dead, https://lalitm.com/post/building-syntaqlite-ai/

Clear human-owned architectural decisions and design intent, especially for the novel, high-value parts of a project where AI defaults are harmful.

## Relations
- → Code Review: "You don’t need to review the code. You review the steps."
- → Scaffold: "I took ownership of all decisions and used it more as “autocomplete on steroids” inside a much tighter process: opinionated design upfront, reviewing every change thoroughly, fixing problems eagerly as I spotted them, and investing in scaffolding"
- ← AI-generated code: "AI’s instinct to normalize was actively harmful there, and those were the parts I had to design in depth and often resorted to just writing myself"
- ← Behavior-Driven Development: "BDD was always a good idea—write specifications in natural language that describe expected behavior, then automate those specs as tests."
- ← Spec-driven development: "In this new paradigm, **Specs become the source of truth**."

# Catastrophic Forgetting
Type: concept
Sources: https://nrehiew.github.io/blog/minimal_editing/

Loss of general coding ability after fine-tuning for minimal editing, especially when training data distribution differs strongly from the model's original distribution.

## Relations
- ← minimal editing training: "whether fine-tuning for minimal editing degrades general coding ability"
- ← RL: "The RL model, however, does not experience any degradation."

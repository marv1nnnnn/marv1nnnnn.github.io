# Scaffold
Type: concept
Sources: https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/scaffold/

A system built around a neural language model that extends its capabilities (e.g., recursive self-calls, REPL interaction, infinite context). The author argues scaffolds ARE the future form of language models, not mere wrappers.

## Relations
- → Neural Language Model: "a powerful class of language models with near-infinite input, output, and reasoning context are scaffolds around neural language models that can call themselves recursively inside of a REPL"
- ← Language Model Definition: "the line between a language model and a scaffold is blurring, and the field is once again open to novel ideas on what these scaffolds should look like"
- ← Neural Language Model: "the language models we interact with in the (near) future will be what we call scaffolds today"
- ← Recursive Language Models: "The argument presented in that paper is a formalized implementation of the theme of this essay"

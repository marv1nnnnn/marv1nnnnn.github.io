# Neural Language Model
Type: concept
Sources: https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/scaffold/

End-to-end neural network models (e.g., Opus 4.6, Qwen3-8B) that map text to text. Traditionally the exclusive focus of scaling efforts, but the author argues they are severely underutilized without scaffolding.

## Relations
- → Scaffold: "the language models we interact with in the (near) future will be what we call scaffolds today"
- → Evaluation Crisis: "How do you begin to evaluate between scaffolds like Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Antigravity with anything other than 'vibes'?"
- ← Bitter Lesson (Scale): "religiously following this bet is how companies like OpenAI and Anthropic have exploded in valuation since"
- ← Scaffold: "a powerful class of language models with near-infinite input, output, and reasoning context are scaffolds around neural language models that can call themselves recursively inside of a REPL"

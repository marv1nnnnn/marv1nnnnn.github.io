# data effects
Type: concept
Sources: https://x.com/danhockenmaier/status/2034698196098978012

A mechanism in AI applications where larger proprietary datasets expand what a product can do, not just how well it does it, strengthening defensibility and scale advantages.

## Relations
- → economic concentration: "For the application layer of AI, the change will come in the form of strengthening data effects, where the larger and more proprietary the dataset, the more value a product can deliver."
- → automated services companies: "It’s a race to aggregate as much data and as many completed jobs as possible to enable a product that can do more work at higher accuracy and lower cost."

# René Bittel
Type: person
Sources: https://paulgraham.com/brandage.html

Head of Patek Philippe's ad agency, credited with positioning the hobnail Calatrava as a brand signature.

## Relations
- → Patek Philippe: "in 1984 Bittel told Patek president Philippe Stern, in effect: make this your standard design, and I'll create an ad campaign to identify it in people's heads with your brand."

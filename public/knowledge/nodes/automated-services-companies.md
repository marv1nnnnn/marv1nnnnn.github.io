# automated services companies
Type: concept
Sources: https://x.com/danhockenmaier/status/2034698196098978012

AI-native firms that use agents to perform service work autonomously, allowing fragmented service industries to consolidate around scalable software-like businesses.

## Relations
- → economic concentration: "Automated services companies will get massive, and in doing so, begin to consolidate the fragmented services landscape we see today."
- ← AI adoption: "Automated services companies will get massive, and in doing so, begin to consolidate the fragmented services landscape we see today."
- ← data effects: "It’s a race to aggregate as much data and as many completed jobs as possible to enable a product that can do more work at higher accuracy and lower cost."

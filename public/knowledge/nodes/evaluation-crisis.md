# Evaluation Crisis
Type: concept
Sources: https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/scaffold/

The inability to meaningfully compare scaffold-based systems (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Antigravity) because benchmarks were designed for raw model capability, not scaffolded systems. A consequence of the 'language model purist' view.

## Relations
- ← Bitter Lesson (Scale): "the obsession over 'raw model capability' has ironically led to our evaluation metrics being completely off"
- ← Neural Language Model: "How do you begin to evaluate between scaffolds like Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Antigravity with anything other than 'vibes'?"

# Intentionality
Type: concept
Sources: https://terriblesoftware.org/2026/01/08/life-happens-at-1x-speed/

Deliberately choosing what to consume based on genuine value rather than optimizing for throughput

## Relations
- → FOMO: "It's about asking what you're trying to get out of all this consumption in the first place. If the answer is just "more," maybe that's worth examining."
- ← 1x Speed Consumption: "if something isn't worth consuming at 1x, it's not worth consuming at all"
- ← Boredom: "It's where our best ideas — like starting this blog! — come from. It's where you actually process what you've learned, make connections, have original thoughts."

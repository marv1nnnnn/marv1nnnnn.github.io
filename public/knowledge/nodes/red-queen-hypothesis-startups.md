# Red Queen Hypothesis (Startups)
Type: concept
Sources: https://registerspill.thorstenball.com/p/joy-and-curiosity-79

Adapted from Leigh Van Valen's evolutionary biology concept: when new startup methods are quickly adopted by everyone, no one gains relative advantage and success rates stay flat. Startups must develop novel, differentiating strategies before competitors catch up.

# aggregators
Type: concept
Sources: https://x.com/danhockenmaier/status/2034698196098978012

Mega-companies that control distribution needed by smaller businesses and therefore capture much of the economic value created by broader market activity.

## Relations
- → economic concentration: "Companies like Google, Meta, and Apple control the distribution that all of these new businesses need in order to grow, and as a result, command much of the newly generated economic value."
- ← distribution bottleneck: "it will not make it easier to get distribution, and that will become the bottleneck, allowing aggregators to extract most of the value."

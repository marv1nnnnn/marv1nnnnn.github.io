# economic concentration
Type: concept
Sources: https://x.com/danhockenmaier/status/2034698196098978012

The long-run concentration of value, revenue, profit, and capital into a small set of top firms, which the essay argues AI will intensify.

## Relations
- ← aggregators: "Companies like Google, Meta, and Apple control the distribution that all of these new businesses need in order to grow, and as a result, command much of the newly generated economic value."
- ← AI adoption: "AI will drive greater concentration of value into fewer companies and create the biggest businesses of all time."
- ← automated services companies: "Automated services companies will get massive, and in doing so, begin to consolidate the fragmented services landscape we see today."
- ← data effects: "For the application layer of AI, the change will come in the form of strengthening data effects, where the larger and more proprietary the dataset, the more value a product can deliver."
- ← internal coordination costs: "AI means many fewer people will be required to produce a unit of output. This will lower headcount, lower complexity, and cause internal coordination costs to plummet."

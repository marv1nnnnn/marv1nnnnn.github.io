# AI adoption
Type: concept
Sources: https://www.latent.space/p/reviews-dead, https://x.com/danhockenmaier/status/2034698196098978012

The adoption of AI is framed as a force that will increase economic concentration rather than fragment markets, creating fewer, larger companies.

## Relations
- → Code Review: "Teams with high AI adoption complete 21% more tasks and merge 98% more pull requests, but PR review time increases 91%, based on [data](https://www.faros.ai/blog/ai-software-engineering) from over 10,000 developers across 1,255 teams."
- → economic concentration: "AI will drive greater concentration of value into fewer companies and create the biggest businesses of all time."
- → internal coordination costs: "AI will do this much more powerfully than legacy software, because it won’t just give humans tools to manage more efficiently, it will do a lot of the work itself."
- → automated services companies: "Automated services companies will get massive, and in doing so, begin to consolidate the fragmented services landscape we see today."
- ← Aviator: "Aviator’s platform helps modern organizations improve AI adoption while maintaining high engineering standards."
- ← Friction as Feature: "There's a feeling that all the things that create friction in your life should be automated away. That human involvement should be replaced by AI-based decision-making. Because it is the friction of the process that is the problem. When in fact many times the friction, or that things just take time, is precisely the point"
- ← Red Queen Hypothesis (Startups): "when new startup methods are quickly adopted by everyone, no one gains a relative advantage, and success rates stay flat"

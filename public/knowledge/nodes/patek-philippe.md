# Patek Philippe
Type: company
Sources: https://paulgraham.com/brandage.html

Prestigious Swiss watchmaker that successfully pivoted toward brand-centric strategy.

## Relations
- → Golden Ellipse: "In 1968 (that year again) Patek Philippe launched a new watch that shifted the center of gravity of case design... The result was a striking new model called the Golden Ellipse."
- → Brand advertising: "To encourage them, Patek did something that none of the holy trinity had done much of before: brand advertising."
- → Gérald Genta: "Encouraged by the success of the Royal Oak, Patek Philippe commissioned Gérald Genta in 1974 to design a similar watch for them."
- → Nautilus: "It was called the Nautilus, and it launched at the Basel Watch Fair in 1976."
- ← René Bittel: "in 1984 Bittel told Patek president Philippe Stern, in effect: make this your standard design, and I'll create an ad campaign to identify it in people's heads with your brand."

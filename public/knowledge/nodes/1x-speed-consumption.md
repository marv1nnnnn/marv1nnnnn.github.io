# 1x Speed Consumption
Type: concept
Sources: https://terriblesoftware.org/2026/01/08/life-happens-at-1x-speed/

Philosophy of consuming all content (podcasts, videos, audiobooks) at normal speed as a quality filter for what deserves attention

## Relations
- → Boredom: "Your brain needs empty space too. This is the part we've collectively forgotten. Boredom is a feature, not a bug."
- → Intentionality: "if something isn't worth consuming at 1x, it's not worth consuming at all"
- ← FOMO: "why do we want to consume things faster? The answer, if we're being honest, usually comes down to FOMO or anxiety (or a mix of both)"

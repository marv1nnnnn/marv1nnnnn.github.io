# Gérald Genta
Type: person
Sources: https://paulgraham.com/brandage.html

Renowned designer commissioned by Audemars Piguet and Patek Philippe for iconic sports-luxury watch designs.

## Relations
- ← Audemars Piguet: "Audemars Piguet, who in 1970 commissioned the renowned designer Gérald Genta to design their own iconic watch"
- ← Patek Philippe: "Encouraged by the success of the Royal Oak, Patek Philippe commissioned Gérald Genta in 1974 to design a similar watch for them."

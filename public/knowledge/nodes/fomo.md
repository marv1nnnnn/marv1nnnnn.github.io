# FOMO
Type: concept
Sources: https://terriblesoftware.org/2026/01/08/life-happens-at-1x-speed/

Fear of missing out — anxiety-driven compulsion to consume more content faster

## Relations
- → 1x Speed Consumption: "why do we want to consume things faster? The answer, if we're being honest, usually comes down to FOMO or anxiety (or a mix of both)"
- ← Intentionality: "It's about asking what you're trying to get out of all this consumption in the first place. If the answer is just "more," maybe that's worth examining."

# SKILL.md files
Type: concept
Sources: https://justin.poehnelt.com/posts/rewrite-your-cli-for-ai-agents/, https://registerspill.thorstenball.com/p/joy-and-curiosity-79

Files that distill a person's preferences, taste, judgment, and experiences into a format consumable by AI models to help them produce work aligned with the author's style and standards.

## Relations
- → Ephemeral Software: "A file as a distillation of one's own preferences and taste and judgement and experiences, fed to a neural network trained to help you get your work done"
- ← Google Workspace CLI: "`gws` ships 100+ `SKILL.md` files"

# Recursive Language Models
Type: concept
Sources: https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/scaffold/

A proposed class of language models that achieve near-infinite input, output, and reasoning context by scaffolding neural LMs with recursive self-calls inside a REPL. Formalized in the author's 2025 preprint.
